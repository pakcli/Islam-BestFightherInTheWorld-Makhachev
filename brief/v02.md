# 2–3 Years Dagestan — Unofficial Fan Meme Archive (v02)

## 1. Concept & Vision
An **unofficial fan archive** celebrating Islam Makhachev's viral internet memes and iconic press conference quotes. Styled as a clean, rounded, light-themed digital timeline and video vault.

## 2. Key Changes in v02
- **Unofficial Fan Vault Persona**: Explicitly framed as a community-driven fan project ("Islam Makhachev Meme Archive").
- **Rounded Light Theme**: Soft neutral background (`#F6F8FA`), ultra-rounded cards (`border-radius: 20px`), clean typography, soft card shadows, and vibrant emerald (`#10B981`) & warm gold (`#F59E0B`) accents.
- **Direct Hero Video Embed**: Featured main hero section embeds the real YouTube video: `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/bgQNGzz9Q_k?start=164`.
- **No Subscribe Button**: Removed commercial subscription UI; replaced with clean fan archive action buttons ("Watch Original Clip", "Explore Timeline").
- **Embedded Media Modals**: Clicking cards opens video modals with live video iframe playback.

## 3. Structure & Layout
1. **Interactive Scrubber Bar**: Light timeline scrubber pinned at top of page spanning 2021 to 2026.
2. **Hero Main Video**: Live YouTube iframe (`bgQNGzz9Q_k&t=164s`) framed in a rounded card.
3. **Chronological Playlist (11 entries)**: Rounded cards for timestamped memes.
4. **Shorts / Clips Row (14 entries)**: Horizontally scrollable row of rounded undated meme cards.
5. **Footer**: Clear fan disclaimer ("Not affiliated with UFC or Islam Makhachev").
